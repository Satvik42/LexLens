EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (REVISED)

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 20 July 2025 between Northwind Analytics Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 4th Floor, Prestige Meridian, Bengaluru 560001 (the "Company"), and Ms. Ananya Rao, residing at 22 Lavelle Road, Bengaluru 560001 (the "Employee").

The Company and the Employee are individually referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

1. Appointment

1.1 The Company hereby appoints the Employee as Senior Data Analyst, and the Employee accepts such appointment on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

1.2 The Employee shall report to the Head of Analytics or such other person as the Company may designate from time to time.

1.3 The Employee's primary place of work shall be the Company's Bengaluru office. The Employee may work remotely for up to three (3) days per week, subject to the Company's remote working policy.

2. Commencement and Probation

2.1 The Employee's employment shall commence on 1 August 2025 (the "Joining Date").

2.2 The Employee shall be on probation for a period of three (3) months from the Joining Date (the "Probation Period").

2.3 Upon successful completion of the Probation Period, the Company shall confirm the Employee's appointment in writing.

3. Duties and Responsibilities

3.1 The Employee shall perform the duties reasonably associated with the position of Senior Data Analyst and such other duties consistent with the position as the Company may assign.

3.2 The Employee shall devote the whole of her working time, attention and skill to the business of the Company and shall not, without the prior written consent of the Company, engage in any other business, employment or consultancy, whether paid or unpaid.

4. Initial Terms of Engagement

4.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving sixty (60) days written notice to the other Party, or by the Company paying salary in lieu of such notice.

4.2 The Employee shall complete the Company's onboarding programme within the first thirty (30) days of the Joining Date.

5. Compensation

5.1 The Employee shall be entitled to an annual cost to company ("CTC") of INR 13,50,000 (Rupees Thirteen Lakh Fifty Thousand only), payable in twelve equal monthly instalments on or before the last working day of each month, subject to applicable statutory deductions.

5.2 The Employee shall be eligible for a discretionary annual performance bonus of up to fifteen percent (15%) of the fixed annual salary, payable at the sole discretion of the Company and subject to the Employee being in employment and not under notice on the payment date.

5.3 The Company shall contribute to the Employee's Provident Fund in accordance with the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952.

5.4 The Employee shall be covered under the Company's group health insurance policy for a sum insured of INR 5,00,000, with effect from the Joining Date.

5.5 The Employee shall be eligible to participate in the Company's Employee Stock Option Plan, subject to a separate grant letter and the terms of the Plan. Unvested options shall lapse on the last working day.

6. Working Hours and Leave

6.1 The Employee's normal working hours shall be 9:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.

6.2 The Employee shall be entitled to twenty (20) days of paid earned leave and twelve (12) days of paid casual or sick leave per calendar year, accruing on a pro-rata basis.

7. Intellectual Property

7.1 All inventions, works of authorship, designs, data, software, know-how and other intellectual property created by the Employee in the course of her employment shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company ("Work Product").

7.2 The Employee hereby assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in and to the Work Product.

8. Termination

8.1 The Company may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect and without notice if the Employee commits any act of gross misconduct, fraud or dishonesty, or is in material breach of this Agreement and fails to remedy such breach within fourteen (14) days of written notice.

8.2 Termination by Notice. Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing sixty (60) days written notice to the other Party. The Company may, at its discretion, waive all or part of the notice period or pay salary in lieu of notice.

8.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination for any reason, the Employee shall immediately return all Company property and complete a handover of her responsibilities to the satisfaction of the Company.

8.4 The Company shall pay the Employee's final settlement within thirty (30) days of the last working day.

9. Confidentiality

9.1 The Employee shall not, during the term of employment or for a period of three (3) years thereafter, use or disclose any Confidential Information to any person, except as required in the proper performance of her duties or as required by law.

10. Restrictive Covenants

10.1 Non-Competition. For a period of six (6) months following the termination of employment for any reason, the Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, be engaged, employed or interested in any business in India that competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of termination.

10.2 Non-Solicitation. For a period of twelve (12) months following termination, the Employee shall not solicit or entice away any employee or customer of the Company with whom she had material dealings during the last twelve (12) months of her employment.

11. Training

11.1 The Company shall enrol the Employee in the Advanced Analytics Certification Programme at the Company's cost. No repayment of training costs shall be required from the Employee.

12. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.

12.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator appointed jointly by the Parties, in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat of arbitration shall be Bengaluru.

13. General

13.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties in relation to the Employee's employment and supersedes all prior discussions, offers and understandings.

13.2 No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the Parties have executed this Agreement on the date first written above.
