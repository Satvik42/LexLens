RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rental Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 1 September 2025 between Mr. Suresh Iyer, residing at 14 Cunningham Road, Bengaluru 560052 (the "Landlord"), and Mr. Karan Mehta, currently residing at 8 Residency Road, Bengaluru 560025 (the "Tenant").

1. Premises

1.1 The Landlord agrees to let, and the Tenant agrees to take on rent, the residential apartment bearing No. 302, Lakeview Residency, Koramangala 4th Block, Bengaluru 560034, comprising two bedrooms, a living room, a kitchen and two bathrooms, together with one covered car parking space (the "Premises").

1.2 The Premises shall be used solely for residential purposes by the Tenant and his immediate family.

2. Term

2.1 The tenancy shall be for a period of eleven (11) months commencing on 1 September 2025 and ending on 31 July 2026 (the "Term").

2.2 The Term may be renewed for a further period on mutually agreed terms, provided the Tenant gives the Landlord written notice of his intention to renew at least sixty (60) days before the end of the Term.

2.3 On renewal, the monthly rent shall be increased by five percent (5%) over the rent payable in the preceding Term.

3. Rent and Deposit

3.1 The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of INR 32,000 (Rupees Thirty-Two Thousand only), payable in advance on or before the fifth (5th) day of each calendar month by bank transfer to the account designated by the Landlord.

3.2 If the rent is not paid within seven (7) days of the due date, the Tenant shall pay a late fee of INR 500 for each day of delay.

3.3 The Tenant has paid to the Landlord an interest-free refundable security deposit of INR 1,60,000 (Rupees One Lakh Sixty Thousand only), receipt of which the Landlord acknowledges.

3.4 The security deposit shall be refunded to the Tenant within thirty (30) days of the Tenant vacating the Premises, after deducting any unpaid rent, utility charges and the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear. The Landlord shall provide an itemised statement of any deductions.

3.5 The Tenant shall additionally pay a monthly maintenance charge of INR 3,500 to the residents' association directly.

4. Lock-in Period

4.1 The Parties agree to a lock-in period of six (6) months from the commencement of the Term (the "Lock-in Period"), during which neither Party shall terminate this Agreement except for breach.

4.2 If the Tenant vacates the Premises during the Lock-in Period, the Tenant shall pay rent for the remaining months of the Lock-in Period, which the Landlord may deduct from the security deposit.

5. Utilities

5.1 The Tenant shall pay for electricity, water, cooking gas, internet and any other utilities consumed at the Premises during the Term.

5.2 The Landlord shall pay all property taxes in respect of the Premises.

6. Maintenance and Repairs

6.1 The Tenant shall keep the Premises in clean and good condition and shall be responsible for minor repairs up to INR 2,000 per instance, including replacement of bulbs, taps and fittings.

6.2 The Landlord shall be responsible for major structural repairs, plumbing and electrical faults not caused by the Tenant, and shall attend to such repairs within a reasonable time of being notified.

6.3 The Tenant shall not make any structural alterations or additions to the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

7. Tenant's Obligations

7.1 The Tenant shall not sublet, assign or part with possession of the Premises or any part thereof.

7.2 The Tenant shall not keep pets in the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

7.3 The Tenant shall not carry on any illegal, commercial or objectionable activity in the Premises and shall not cause nuisance to neighbours.

7.4 The Tenant shall permit the Landlord or his representative to inspect the Premises at reasonable times on at least twenty-four (24) hours prior notice.

7.5 The Tenant shall comply with the rules and bye-laws of the residents' association.

8. Termination

8.1 After the Lock-in Period, either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving the other Party two (2) months written notice.

8.2 The Landlord may terminate this Agreement by giving fifteen (15) days written notice if the Tenant fails to pay rent for two (2) consecutive months or commits a material breach of this Agreement that is not remedied within fifteen (15) days of written notice.

8.3 On expiry or termination, the Tenant shall hand over vacant and peaceful possession of the Premises, together with all fixtures and fittings, in the same condition as at commencement, subject to normal wear and tear.

9. Governing Law

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the Parties have signed this Agreement on the date first written above.

Landlord: Suresh Iyer

Tenant: Karan Mehta
