EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 1 July 2025 between Northwind Analytics Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 4th Floor, Prestige Meridian, Bengaluru 560001 (the "Company"), and Ms. Ananya Rao, residing at 22 Lavelle Road, Bengaluru 560001 (the "Employee").

The Company and the Employee are individually referred to as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

1. Appointment

1.1 The Company hereby appoints the Employee as Senior Data Analyst, and the Employee accepts such appointment on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

1.2 The Employee shall report to the Head of Analytics or such other person as the Company may designate from time to time.

1.3 The Employee's primary place of work shall be the Company's Bengaluru office. The Company may require the Employee to travel within India for business purposes on reasonable notice.

2. Commencement and Probation

2.1 The Employee's employment shall commence on 15 July 2025 (the "Joining Date").

2.2 The Employee shall be on probation for a period of six (6) months from the Joining Date (the "Probation Period"). The Company may, at its discretion, extend the Probation Period by up to three (3) months by written notice to the Employee.

2.3 Upon successful completion of the Probation Period, the Company shall confirm the Employee's appointment in writing.

3. Duties and Responsibilities

3.1 The Employee shall perform the duties reasonably associated with the position of Senior Data Analyst and such other duties consistent with the position as the Company may assign.

3.2 The Employee shall devote the whole of her working time, attention and skill to the business of the Company and shall not, without the prior written consent of the Company, engage in any other business, employment or consultancy, whether paid or unpaid.

3.3 The Employee shall comply with all lawful and reasonable instructions of the Company and with the Company's policies, as amended from time to time.

4. Initial Terms of Engagement

4.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days written notice to the other Party, or by the Company paying salary in lieu of such notice.

4.2 The Employee shall complete the Company's onboarding programme within the first thirty (30) days of the Joining Date.

4.3 The Employee shall provide the Company with all documents reasonably required to verify her identity, qualifications and prior employment within seven (7) days of the Joining Date.

5. Compensation

5.1 The Employee shall be entitled to an annual cost to company ("CTC") of INR 12,00,000 (Rupees Twelve Lakh only), payable in twelve equal monthly instalments on or before the last working day of each month, subject to applicable statutory deductions.

5.2 The Employee shall be eligible for a discretionary annual performance bonus of up to ten percent (10%) of the fixed annual salary, payable at the sole discretion of the Company and subject to the Employee being in employment and not under notice on the payment date.

5.3 The Company shall contribute to the Employee's Provident Fund in accordance with the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952.

5.4 The Employee shall be covered under the Company's group health insurance policy for a sum insured of INR 5,00,000, with effect from the Joining Date.

5.5 The Company shall reimburse reasonable, pre-approved business expenses incurred by the Employee in the performance of her duties, on submission of valid receipts within thirty (30) days of incurring the expense.

5.6 The Company shall review the Employee's compensation annually. Any revision shall be at the sole discretion of the Company.

6. Working Hours and Leave

6.1 The Employee's normal working hours shall be 9:30 a.m. to 6:30 p.m., Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. The Employee may be required to work additional hours as reasonably necessary for the proper performance of her duties without additional remuneration.

6.2 The Employee shall be entitled to eighteen (18) days of paid earned leave and twelve (12) days of paid casual or sick leave per calendar year, accruing on a pro-rata basis.

6.3 Unused earned leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of thirty (30) days. Leave shall not be encashed except on separation from the Company in accordance with Company policy.

6.4 Leave shall be taken at times convenient to the Company and subject to prior approval of the Employee's reporting manager.

7. Intellectual Property

7.1 All inventions, works of authorship, designs, data, software, know-how and other intellectual property created by the Employee in the course of her employment, whether alone or with others, and whether or not during working hours, shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company ("Work Product").

7.2 The Employee hereby assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in and to the Work Product, and shall execute such further documents as the Company may reasonably require to perfect such assignment.

7.3 The Employee waives all moral rights in the Work Product to the fullest extent permitted by law.

8. Termination

8.1 The Company may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect and without notice or payment in lieu of notice if the Employee (a) commits any act of gross misconduct, fraud or dishonesty; (b) is in material breach of this Agreement and fails to remedy such breach within fourteen (14) days of written notice; (c) is convicted of any criminal offence involving moral turpitude; or (d) is in persistent breach of the Company's policies.

8.2 Termination by Notice. Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing sixty (60) days written notice to the other Party. The Company may, at its discretion, waive all or part of the notice period or pay salary in lieu of notice.

8.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination for any reason, the Employee shall (a) immediately return all Company property, including laptops, access cards, documents and data in any form; (b) complete a handover of her responsibilities to the satisfaction of the Company; and (c) continue to comply with the confidentiality, intellectual property and restrictive covenant obligations under this Agreement.

8.4 The Company shall pay the Employee's final settlement, including accrued salary and encashable leave, within forty-five (45) days of the last working day, subject to the Employee's compliance with Clause 8.3.

9. Confidentiality

9.1 The Employee acknowledges that during her employment she will have access to Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means all information relating to the business, customers, suppliers, products, pricing, technology, data, finances and affairs of the Company or its group companies that is not publicly available.

9.2 The Employee shall not, during the term of employment or at any time thereafter, use or disclose any Confidential Information to any person, except as required in the proper performance of her duties or as required by law.

9.3 The obligations in this Clause 9 shall survive the termination of this Agreement without limit in time.

10. Restrictive Covenants

10.1 Non-Competition. For a period of twelve (12) months following the termination of employment for any reason, the Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, be engaged, employed or interested in any business in India that competes with the business of the Company as carried on at the date of termination.

10.2 Non-Solicitation of Employees. For a period of twelve (12) months following termination, the Employee shall not solicit or entice away, or attempt to solicit or entice away, any employee of the Company with whom she had material dealings during the last twelve (12) months of her employment.

10.3 Non-Solicitation of Customers. For a period of twelve (12) months following termination, the Employee shall not solicit or deal with any customer or client of the Company with whom she had material dealings during the last twelve (12) months of her employment.

10.4 The Employee acknowledges that the restrictions in this Clause 10 are reasonable and necessary to protect the legitimate business interests of the Company.

11. Training and Repayment of Training Costs

11.1 The Company shall enrol the Employee in the Advanced Analytics Certification Programme (the "Training") at the Company's cost, which the Parties acknowledge is INR 2,00,000 (Rupees Two Lakh only).

11.2 If the Employee resigns from employment, or is terminated under Clause 8.1, within eighteen (18) months of completing the Training, the Employee shall repay to the Company the Training cost of INR 2,00,000 within thirty (30) days of the last working day.

11.3 The Company may set off any amount repayable under Clause 11.2 against the Employee's final settlement.

12. Data Protection

12.1 The Employee consents to the Company collecting, storing and processing her personal data for purposes connected with her employment, in accordance with applicable law and the Company's privacy policy.

12.2 The Employee shall comply with the Company's data protection and information security policies in handling any personal data of customers, employees or other individuals.

13. Notices

13.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, registered post or email to the addresses set out above or such other address as a Party may notify.

13.2 Notices delivered by email shall be deemed received on the next business day after transmission.

14. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

14.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.

14.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator appointed by the Company, in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat of arbitration shall be Bengaluru.

14.3 Subject to Clause 14.2, the courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

15. General

15.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties in relation to the Employee's employment and supersedes all prior discussions, offers and understandings.

15.2 No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.

15.3 If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the Parties have executed this Agreement on the date first written above.
